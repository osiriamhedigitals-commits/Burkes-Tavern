About Burke's Tavern

Where Fine Dining Meets Timeless Elegance

Nestled in the heart of Newark, New Jersey, Burke's Tavern is more than just a restaurant. It is an experience.

For over a decade, we have been crafting unforgettable moments around the table. Our mission is simple: to bring people together through exceptional food, curated drinks, and service that feels like home.

From the moment you step through our doors, you are welcomed into a world of black, gold, and warm light. A world where every detail matters. The music, the seating, the way your wine glass catches the candlelight. We designed Burke's to be the place for first dates, business deals, family celebrations, and those nights when you just deserve something extraordinary.

At Burke's, we believe great food starts with great ingredients. Our executive chef works with local farms and premium suppliers to create a menu that honors classic American and global cuisine, with a modern twist.

We don’t just feed you. We create the backdrop for your most important moments.
Welcome to Burke's. Welcome home.
